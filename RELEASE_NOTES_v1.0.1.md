# WHEN-TO-WAKE ABI v1.0.1

Released: 2026-08-03

## Scope

This patch release corrects spacing and layer separation in the graphical abstract generated by `code/build_publication_assets.py`.

## Scientific invariance

Version 1.0.1 makes no change to:

- cohort construction or eligibility;
- exposure or outcome definitions;
- target-trial time zero, grace period, or estimand;
- MIMIC-IV or eICU-CRD aggregate results;
- bootstrap estimates, balance diagnostics, or sensitivity analyses;
- the frozen protocol, statistical analysis plan, amendments, or novelty audit.

The published numerical results therefore remain identical to v1.0.0. Patient-level data are not included.
