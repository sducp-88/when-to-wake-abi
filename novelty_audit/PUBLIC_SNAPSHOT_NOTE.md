# Public novelty-audit contents

The public audit preserves:

- the search databases and exact query strings;
- retrieval timestamps and reported/retrieved counts;
- the human screening decision ledger for closest neighbors;
- the bounded novelty report.

The bulk PubMed, Europe PMC, and ClinicalTrials.gov API inventories are retained in the private project audit archive. They are omitted here because they contain large amounts of third-party abstract or registry text and can be recreated from the dated search logs. Their omission does not change the reported counts or screening ledger.
