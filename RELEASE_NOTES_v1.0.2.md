# WHEN-TO-WAKE ABI v1.0.2

Released: 2026-08-03

Archived version DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21766624

## Scope

This presentation-only patch harmonizes the journal-facing figures and graphical abstract generated by `code/build_publication_assets.py`.

Changes include:

- a restrained, colorblind-conscious blue-gray and teal palette;
- removal of redundant in-figure titles and slide-like explanatory text;
- direct display of risk differences and 95% confidence intervals without p-value labels;
- clearer visual separation of the primary, transport, sensitivity, and exploratory estimates;
- a compact covariate-balance display restricted to the 18 largest post-weighting values in each database, with full numeric diagnostics retained in the aggregate files;
- RGB, LZW-compressed 600-dpi TIFF exports plus PNG, PDF, and SVG outputs.

## Scientific invariance

Version 1.0.2 makes no change to:

- cohort construction or eligibility;
- exposure or outcome definitions;
- target-trial time zero, grace period, or estimand;
- MIMIC-IV or eICU-CRD aggregate results;
- bootstrap estimates, balance diagnostics, or sensitivity analyses;
- the protocol, statistical analysis plan, amendments, or novelty audit.

Patient-level data are not included.
