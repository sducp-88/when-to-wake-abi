# APACHE IVa time-valid sensitivity feasibility audit

This post hoc audit was undertaken after the primary results were available in response to a severity-confounding review. It did not redefine the primary MIMIC-IV estimand.

To avoid using measurements collected after an early treatment decision, the exploratory eICU model was restricted to complete-case APACHE IVa decision points at least 1,440 minutes after ICU admission. APACHE IVa score was added to the adherence denominator model, and outcome-blind calibration used baseline clinical margins.

The restricted analysis retained 269 continued-strategy clones from 134 stays and 181 early-strategy clones from 128 stays. The maximum absolute weighted standardized mean difference was 0.1198, exceeding the prespecified 0.10 gate, and the early-strategy effective-sample-size ratio was 0.4876, below the prespecified 0.50 gate. The maximum calibrated weight remained below 20.

Because two feasibility gates failed, this model is noninferential. Its effect estimate and confidence interval are not reported or used to support the manuscript. APACHE IVa is instead reported as an outcome-blind baseline severity-balance diagnostic in the full GCS-corrected eICU transport analysis.
