# WHEN-TO-WAKE ABI v1.0.3

Released: 2026-08-03

Archived version DOI: assigned after release; the concept DOI is https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21761953

## Scope

This presentation-only patch regularizes the geometry of the Figure 1 cohort-flow diagram generated by `code/build_publication_assets.py`.

Changes include:

- a common fixed width and height for every cohort-flow box;
- equal vertical center spacing across both database columns;
- connector endpoints computed from the fixed box boundaries;
- identical visible connector length, arrowhead size, line weight, and clearance for all eight arrows;
- preservation of the restrained colorblind-conscious palette and RGB, LZW-compressed 600-dpi TIFF export.

## Scientific invariance

Version 1.0.3 makes no change to:

- cohort construction or eligibility;
- exposure or outcome definitions;
- target-trial time zero, grace period, or estimand;
- MIMIC-IV or eICU-CRD aggregate results;
- bootstrap estimates, balance diagnostics, or sensitivity analyses;
- the protocol, statistical analysis plan, amendments, or novelty audit.

Patient-level data are not included.
