# Data-use and disclosure notice

This repository contains no MIMIC-IV or eICU-CRD source records and no patient-level derivative dataset. The included CSV and JSON files are disclosure-reviewed aggregate estimates, diagnostic summaries, bootstrap replicate summaries, or public literature/registry screening records.

Access to MIMIC-IV and eICU-CRD is governed by PhysioNet and the respective data-use requirements. Users must obtain their own authorized access. The repository's eventual software license will not grant rights to the source databases.

Do not open an issue containing patient-level values, credentials, local data paths, or screenshots of controlled records.
